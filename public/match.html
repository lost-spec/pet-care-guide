Tell us about your household

Twelve questions about where you live and how you spend your week. There are no wrong answers, and being honest will get you a more useful result.

1. What sort of home is it?
2. What outdoor space is there?
3. On a typical weekday, how long is the house empty?
4. How active is your household?
5. How much experience do you have with this kind of pet?
6. Who lives with you?
7. How much indoor space do you have?
8. How well does your home tolerate noise?
9. How much grooming are you happy to do?
10. What is your budget like?
11. How much time can you give a pet each week?
12. What is the main reason you want a pet?
