Worth knowing: toxic foods & hazards

Lilies
Pollen and leaves from true lilies can cause sudden kidney failure.
Chocolate
Toxic, especially dark chocolate and baking chocolate.
Onions & garlic
Toxic to cats in powder, raw and cooked forms.
Alcohol & caffeine
Small amounts can be dangerous.
String & thread
Swallowed string can wind around the intestine and needs urgent care.
Getting one from a breeder or marketplace? Shelters and rescues come first. If you use a breeder or marketplace listing, verify the seller before paying: ask to see the parents, request written health and vaccination records, avoid sellers who will not let you visit, and be wary of puppy-mill listings and payment requests made through chat or wire transfer.
