Buying from a breeder or marketplace? Verify the seller before paying. Ask to see the parents, request written health and vaccination records, avoid sellers who will not let you visit, and be wary of puppy-mill listings and payment requests made through chat or wire transfer. Adopting from a shelter or rescue is usually the safer option.
